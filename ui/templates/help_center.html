Help Center

Here are some common questions & quick assistance for using AI Matcher.

📌 How does resume–job matching work?

We compare Sentence-BERT embeddings of your resume with each JD. Skills are extracted & matched token-wise.

📌 Why are missing skills empty sometimes?

When JD doesn’t contain technical keywords or your resume matches most requirements.

📌 Why do I see repeated history in reports?

Each JD inside a PDF counts as one record. Multi-JD = multi entries.

📌 Resume Enhancer showing similar tips?

Results depend on real text inside resume. If strong profile → fewer suggestions 👍

📌 How to increase match percentage?

  • Add relevant tools/tech stack
  • Use bullet points instead of paragraphs
  • Mention measurable results & achievements

📌 Why rewritten summary short?

Rewrite currently extracts best points | Expanded version coming soon 🚀

📌 How to clear job report history?

Job Reports → 🗑 Clear History → Clears only your stored comparison data.

📌 PDF Upload Error?

Ensure **PDF format only**. Image-scanned PDFs may fail to extract text.
